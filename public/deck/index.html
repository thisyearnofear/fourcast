01 · THE CLAIM

Every AI trading agent claims an edge.
Almost none can prove their discipline.

/ 01

Unverifiable agents

Autonomous traders publish wins and bury losses. There is no cryptographic record of what the agent decided, when, or under what mandate.

/ 02

Trust-based settlement

Prediction markets settle through operators and APIs you must trust. Results arrive as claims, not proofs.

/ 03

No ex-ante accountability

Nobody can check an agent's reasoning before the outcome — so "the AI said so" is the entire audit trail.

Fourcast inverts this: the decision is committed on-chain first, the oracle proves the result, and settlement is a program — not a promise.

02 · THE SYSTEM

One agent. Five on-chain gates. Zero trust required.

GATE 01

Decide

LLM pipeline finds edge across prediction venues.

GATE 02

Gate

Policy-bound mandate: sizing, exposure, venue rules.

GATE 03

Seal

Decision receipt committed via Solana Memo — before kickoff.

GATE 04

Settle

Anchor program CPIs into TxLINE oracle; Merkle proof of the real result.

GATE 05

Receipt

Payout or refund in a single slot. No admin key. Publicly auditable.

Every claim is verifiable read-only on the Solana explorer — the product's proof page walks the full chain.

03 · WHY SOLANA

Solana isn't the payment rail.
It's the settlement and verification layer.

~1s

Finality for escrow

Parametric escrow settles the moment the oracle proves the result — no challenge window, no operator.

<¢

Sub-cent micro-policies

Per-fixture escrow at fees that make micro-stakes economically viable — impossible on L1s.

CPI

Composable verification

The escrow program calls TxLINE's oracle in the same transaction: validate_fixture → validate_stat_v4 → close.

Plus the two primitives nobody else uses this way: Memo for ex-ante decision commitments and x402 for machine-native payments.
