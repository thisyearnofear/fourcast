# HackCanton user validation

This document records validation for the private CBTC settlement wedge. No
interviews have been recorded in the repository yet; do not present the
thresholds below as achieved evidence.

## Target participants

Interview both sides of the workflow:

1. High-volume prediction-market traders, OTC participants, or liquidity
   providers who split wallets, iceberg orders, or avoid showing full size.
2. Prediction-market venues, trading desks, or market operators who want to
   attract that volume while retaining the visibility needed for settlement,
   risk, and compliance.

## Interview protocol

Do not lead with the demo. Ask:

1. Tell me about the last time another trader seeing your position changed how
   you executed.
2. What do you do today to hide size or direction?
3. What does that workaround cost in time, slippage, fees, or missed size?
4. Which information must remain private, and from whom?
5. Who still needs visibility for settlement, risk, or compliance?
6. Would you accept the venue/operator seeing the position if the public could
   not?
7. Would you collateralize this position in CBTC? Why or why not?
8. What would stop you from testing one position on DevNet?
9. If this worked, who would pay: you, the venue, or the market operator?

After the questions, show the privacy proof and ask whether they would test it
or introduce a suitable tester.

## Validation threshold

The wedge is supported only if:

- 3 of 5 participants describe position leakage as a recurring problem.
- 2 of 5 currently use a costly workaround.
- 2 of 5 agree to test or introduce a tester.
- At least one or two anonymized quotes can be used in the pitch.

If traders describe privacy as merely nice-to-have, stop claiming product-market
validation and investigate private OTC event-risk settlement for desks instead.
